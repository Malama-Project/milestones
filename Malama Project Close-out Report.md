# Malama Project – Project Catalyst Fund 12 Close-out Report (PCR)

**Project Name:** Mālama Project – Distributed Hawaii Carbon Credits on Cardano  
**Project Number:** 1200230  
**IdeaScale URL:** [Malama Project on Catalyst](https://projectcatalyst.io/funds/12/cardano-use-cases-concept/the-malama-project-distributed-hawaii-carbon-credits-on-cardano)  
**Project Manager:** Jeffrey Wise (CEO)  
**Date Project Started:** September 2024  
**Date Project Completed:** April 2025  

---

## Challenge KPIs and How the Project Addressed Them

- **Blockchain Adoption by Smallholders:**  
  - Conducted targeted workshops with Hawaiian farmers to drive real-world blockchain adoption.

- **Democratizing Carbon Credit Access:**  
  - Delivered a fully functional beta platform ([app.malamaproject.org](https://app.malamaproject.org)) for direct carbon credit minting on Cardano.

- **Transparency and Accountability:**  
  - Implemented CIP-68 NFTs for on-chain verification, with CardanoScan integration providing transparent credit tracking.

---

## Project KPIs and How the Project Addressed Them

- **Public Beta Platform Launch:**  
  - Successfully launched the beta platform, incorporating direct farmer feedback.

- **Community Engagement and Feedback Integration:**  
  - Hosted workshops and gathered structured feedback leading to clearly documented, farmer-approved platform enhancements.

- **Robust Documentation:**  
  - Maintained comprehensive public documentation accessible via [Malama Project Milestones](https://github.com/Malama-Project/milestones).

---

## Key Achievements (Collaboration and Engagement)

- **In-person Workshops:**  
  - Facilitated meaningful engagement, gaining insights into local agricultural practices, directly shaping platform improvements.

- **Farmer-Driven Platform Enhancements:**  
  - Implemented specific farmer-requested features ("Micro-Piling," "Soil Testing," simplified data-entry system).

- **Educational Resource Creation:**  
  - Launched [malamacarbon.com](https://www.malamacarbon.com), addressing unique educational needs about blockchain and carbon credits.

- **IoT Sensor Integration:**  
  - Deployed IoT sensors transmitting environmental data directly to the Cardano blockchain.

- **Progress towards Puro Certification:**  
  - Significantly advanced the project's Puro carbon-credit pre-certification status.

---

## Key Learnings

- **Funding and Market Volatility:**  
  - Experienced significant funding challenges due to ADA's market volatility, restricting available resources and travel opportunities.

- **Reviewer Expectations:**  
  - Managed evolving reviewer expectations by closely adhering to defined scope and strategic planning, despite budget constraints.

- **Communication Gap & Education:**  
  - Recognized the importance of clear, accessible educational materials, leading directly to the creation of the dedicated educational website.

---

## Next Steps for Product/Service Developed

- **Cross-chain Minting Integration:**  
  - Developing functionality to enable credit minting across multiple blockchains, utilizing Cardano as the oracle and source of truth.

- **Decentralized Identity (DID) and KYC Implementation:**  
  - Integrating secure DID and KYC systems for farmer identification and regulatory compliance.

- **Expanded IoT Sensor Deployment:**  
  - Scaling sensor deployments across additional farms to enhance environmental data collection and credit issuance.

- **SAFE Funding Round:**  
  - Currently raising additional capital to support expansion, educational outreach, and increased resilience against crypto-market volatility.

---

## Final Thoughts & Comments

The Mālama Project successfully demonstrated the practical integration of blockchain technology within Hawaii’s agricultural sector, setting the groundwork for a sustainable, transparent carbon-credit ecosystem. Collaborative community engagement and adaptive learning have proven essential. We extend our sincere gratitude to Project Catalyst, the Cardano community, and all stakeholders for making this impactful journey possible.
